Winter things that affect plans

  • Charles Fort is closed Mondays in winter, so Cork and Kinsale are on Tuesday.
  • Rock of Cashel last admission is 3:45 PM. Blarney last admission is 4 PM.
  • Dolphin boat tours are seasonal, so that stop is now optional.
  • Daylight ends around 5:30 PM. Drive times in the dark are longer.
  • Extra car cost: Irish rental companies often add a fee for drivers under 25 or over 75, and an International Driving Permit is recommended for some licenses.
