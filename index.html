Know Before You Go

A few things that make the trip smoother.

Driving on the left

Ireland drives on the left and many roads are narrow, especially in Kerry. Rental cars are often manual, so book an automatic early if you want one. Take the first day slowly.

Dublin tolls

The M50 around Dublin uses an electronic toll with no booths. Ask Hertz how it is handled, and pay by the deadline so you do not get a fee.

February weather

Expect cool, damp days, wind and short daylight. Pack waterproof shoes, a rain jacket and warm layers. Sunset is around 5:30 PM, so plan your driving around it.

Money and tipping

Ireland uses the euro and cards are widely accepted. Tipping is not required but 10 to 12 percent is common in sit down restaurants for good service.

Power

Outlets are 230 volts with three prong plugs, so bring adapters. Most phone and laptop chargers handle the voltage, but check yours.

Winter closures

Some sights close early or have shorter winter hours. Each stop page lists the hours I found. Check the day before, especially in bad weather.
