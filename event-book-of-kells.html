Trinity College and the Book of Kells

Thu, Feb 18, Midday

An 8th century illuminated Gospel manuscript and the Long Room library.

Official info and ticketsOpen in Google Maps

The story

The story

Trinity College was founded in 1592 by Elizabeth I. The Book of Kells is a richly decorated copy of the four Gospels made around 800 AD, mostly by Columban monks.

What you will see

The manuscript and the Long Room, about 65 metres long and holding some 200,000 of the oldest books in the library. The Experience ticket adds a digital exhibition.

About the manuscript

The Book of Kells is a Latin copy of the four Gospels, beautifully decorated with intricate patterns, animals and figures. It is named for the monastery at Kells in County Meath and has been at Trinity since the 17th century. Pages are turned periodically, so what is on display changes.

The Long Room

This grand library hall has a barrel vaulted ceiling, dark wooden shelves and busts of famous writers and thinkers. It also houses the Trinity College Harp, often called the Brian Boru harp, and one of the surviving original copies of the 1916 Proclamation.

Tips

This is one of Dublin's busiest attractions. A booked time slot saves waiting, and the display case area is crowded, so give yourself a little extra time.
