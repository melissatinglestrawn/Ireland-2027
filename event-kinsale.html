Charles Fort and Kinsale

Tue, Feb 16, Late afternoon to dinner

A star shaped fort over the harbor, then dinner in a brightly painted food town.

The story

The story

Charles Fort was built in the 1670s as a star fort guarding Kinsale Harbour. Kinsale itself is known for the Battle of Kinsale in 1601 and, much later, for being the nearest harbor to the Lusitania sinking in 1915.

Important winter note

In winter Charles Fort is closed on Mondays. That is why Cork and Kinsale are scheduled on a Tuesday in this version of the plan.

Kinsale town

Wander the colored streets, then dinner at Bulman's Bar or Jim Edwards.

The Battle of Kinsale

In 1601 an English army defeated Irish forces and their Spanish allies outside Kinsale. It is remembered as a turning point that helped end the old Gaelic order, and it shaped Irish history for centuries.

Charles Fort

The fort is a star shaped design with thick walls and long views over the harbor entrance. Walk the ramparts and the old barracks, and look across to James Fort on the other side of the water.

Kinsale town

Kinsale likes to call itself Ireland's gourmet capital, and its narrow colorful streets are made for wandering. The nearby Old Head of Kinsale is where the Lusitania sank in 1915, and that history is still part of the town's story.
